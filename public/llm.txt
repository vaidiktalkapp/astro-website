# VaidikTalk - LLM Context

VaidikTalk is India's most trusted online platform for authentic Vedic Astrology, Online Pujas, and Spiritual Guidance.

## Purpose & Services:
1. **Online Pujas**: We offer authentic Vedic pujas performed live by verified Pandits at sacred locations. Devotees can book online, provide their Name and Gotra for a personal Sankalp, join via a secure live video link, and receive blessed Prasad at their doorstep. 
2. **Astrology Consultations**: Users can consult with India's top-rated, verified astrologers via Call or Chat for personalized guidance on Marriage, Career, Finance, Health, and more.
3. **AI Astrologer**: We provide 24/7 astrological guidance using advanced AI models trained on ancient Vedic wisdom.
4. **Premium Astrology Reports**: We offer highly detailed, graphically-rich PDF reports including the 10-Year Smart Kundali, Kundali Matching, Lal Kitab, Hastlikhit Kundali, and Numerology.
5. **Free Tools**: Users can access Daily Horoscopes, Panchang, Moon Sign calculators, and Planetary Transit information for free.
