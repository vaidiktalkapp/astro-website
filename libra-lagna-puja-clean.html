About this Temple

Gaj Lakshmi Temple, located in Ujjain, is an ancient temple dedicated to Goddess Lakshmi in her Gaj Lakshmi form. According to Hindu beliefs, Gaj Lakshmi is the goddess of wealth, prosperity, good fortune, and abundance. This form of Goddess Lakshmi appeared during the Samudra Manthan, where two elephants are shown performing a water abhishek on her, symbolising prosperity and shubhata. Devotees visit this temple to pray for financial growth, business progress, and family happiness. Special prayers are performed during festivals and Deepawali.
